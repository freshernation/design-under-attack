# Stuck log

Every time you are stuck for more than twenty minutes. One block each, newest at the
bottom. Two minutes to write.

This is not marked and it is not busywork. Your instructor reads it before every live
hour, and it is the difference between an hour spent on what you actually need and an
hour spent on what they guessed you needed.

An honest amber gets you help. A blank log gets you asked why it is blank.

---

```
date:
week/day:
what I was trying to do:
what I could not work out:
how long before I opened a role:
which role (tutor / librarian / roommate / none):
what it turned out to be:
resolved (yes/no):
```

---

## Example

```
date: 2026-09-03
week/day: 1/2
what I was trying to do: write the envelope for the photo service
what I could not work out: whether "photos must never be lost" is a durability
  requirement or a wish
how long before I opened a role: 25 min
which role: tutor
what it turned out to be: it is a wish until you say what failure it survives.
  "survives one machine dying" and "survives a region burning down" are different
  systems and different budgets. I had not noticed I was choosing.
resolved: yes
```
