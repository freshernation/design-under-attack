## The exercise

Three timed designs, forty-five minutes each, on briefs you have not seen. Use
`ai/interviewer.md` — it holds the clock and introduces a twist at minute thirty.

**Announce your budget in the first minute, every time.** It is the single most effective
thing in the article and it takes eleven seconds:

> "I'll spend about five minutes on requirements and scale, then the data model and main
> request paths, then the interesting decisions, and I want to leave ten minutes for
> failure modes. Stop me if you'd rather go somewhere else."

Then score each with `week-12/day-4/scoring.py` and keep the scores. Friday compares your
first to your tenth.

---

## The thing to practise deliberately

**Arithmetic out loud.** Sixty seconds, rounding aggressively, saying the rounding, ending
with what the numbers told you:

> "…so reads fit on a handful of machines and the interesting problem is somewhere else."

That last sentence is what demonstrates you know why you did the arithmetic. Practise it
until it is automatic, because under pressure it is the first thing that goes.

---

## The sentences to have ready

- *"I'll assume X — tell me if that's wrong."*
- *"I'll come back to that."* — and do
- *"That's a bigger question than we have time for — the short version is…"*
- *"Before I go further, is this the part you want?"*

The last one is worth more than the other three combined, and it belongs at about minute
twenty.
