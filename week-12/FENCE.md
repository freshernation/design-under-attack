# Week 12 — Concept fence

## Allowed

Everything. There has been no fence since week 11 and there is none now.

## The only rule left

**Say which of your claims are sourced, which are inferred, and which you are guessing.**

Twelve weeks of the source doctrine come down to one habit, and it is the one that survives
into a real conversation. An interviewer who hears

> "Slack's 2023 post says channel servers map to channels by consistent hashing; it doesn't
> say what happens to in-flight messages during a handover — my guess is clients refetch,
> but that's me"

learns more about you in fifteen seconds than any architecture recital would tell them.

It is also the habit that makes you useful to work with, which outlasts every interview.
