## The exercise

The eighteen explanations in the article. Out loud, timed, ideally while walking.

Four parts each, in under a minute:

1. The problem it solves, in one sentence, no jargon
2. How it works, in two or three sentences
3. **What it costs**
4. **When you would not use it**

Parts 3 and 4 are what distinguish the answer. Anybody can recite a mechanism; a person who
names its cost has used it, and a person who names where it does not apply has thought
about it.

---

## Record three, and listen back

You will hear which ones you have understood and which you have memorised. The difference
is audible in speech in a way it is not on paper: a memorised answer has no number, no
example, and no cost in it.

For any that sound recited, go back to the primary source from that week rather than to
your notes.
