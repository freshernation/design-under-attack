## What is different

**The brief is one line.** Every other brief in this course had a table of numbers in it,
and that was scaffolding. Pass 1 is now the hardest part of the week, and the most common
failure is a document whose requirements were clearly written after the boxes were drawn.

**You build the reviewer.** Tuesday's lab is a linter for design documents — the tells you
have been collecting since week 1, as a program. Then you run it on your own week-8 project,
which is more persuasive than any checklist.

**The decision library is a deliverable.** Every priced decision from eleven weeks, on one
page, grouped by shape rather than by week. It is what you take out of this course, and it
is what you revise from next week.

---

## The one idea

**A design is a set of decisions, each with a reason, a rejected alternative, and a price.**

That was the first sentence of week 1, and it is still the whole thing. Everything since
has been material for making those decisions well: arithmetic to size them, mechanisms to
choose between, failure modes to weigh, and a discipline about which claims you are
entitled to make.

---

## What this week is not about

Being finished. The document will feel thin on Thursday and the temptation is to add a
component.

Do not. **The fastest improvement available on Thursday is deleting the straw-man
alternative and replacing it with an honest one.** A design that admits what it gave up is
much harder to attack than one that only lists what it enables — and that is the document
you want in front of you in week 12.
