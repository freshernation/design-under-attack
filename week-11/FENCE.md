# Week 11 — Concept fence

## Allowed

**Everything.** There is no fence this week.

Eleven weeks of deliberately withholding things has ended. You may name any product, use
any mechanism, and reach for anything in the course or outside it.

## The rules that remain

Three, and they are the ones that were never about limiting you:

**A named product comes with the property you need from it.** The week-6 replacement for
the ban. `design_lint.bare_technology_names` enforces it, and you wrote that function.

**Every number is derived or labelled as assumed.** With a consequence attached. An
assumption without a consequence is decoration.

**Every claim about a real system has a source, with a tier and a date.** Or it is marked
as inferred, or as unknown. `tools/check_sources.py` still runs.

---

## The test that has not changed since week 1

> **Delete every proper noun from your design. Is there anything left?**

If the document collapses into nothing, you had a shopping list. If it survives — if the
decisions, the numbers and the prices are all still there and only the labels are gone —
you have a design, and the proper nouns were the last thing you chose rather than the
first.

Eleven weeks was mostly about making that true.
